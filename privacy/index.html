Moments Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 Contact: momentsapk.app@gmail.com

Moments is a private photo-sharing app for two people. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you can delete it.

The short version

  • Your photos are shared only with the one partner you link to. There is no public feed, no followers and no ads.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.
  • You can delete your account and all your photos at any time from Settings > Delete account.

What we collect

Data Why we need it
Email address To sign you in with a one-time code and to let your partner find you when linking.
Display name and optional partner nickname Shown to you and your partner. The nickname is visible only to you.
Photos and captions you send To show them to your partner and keep them in your shared gallery.
Hearts, pings and social-battery level To deliver these features to your partner.
Time zone To decide which of the three daily slots is open for you.
Purchase status Whether you or your partner has Moments Pro (managed through Google Play and RevenueCat).
Notification token (when notifications are enabled) To send you reminders and alerts about your partner’s activity.
App preferences (accent colour, notification settings, quiet hours) To personalise the app.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising ID. The camera is used only when you take a photo; the photo picker is used only for photos you choose.

Who can see your data

  • Your linked partner sees the photos, captions, hearts, pings, name and email you share within the couple.
  • No one else in the app can see your content. Access is enforced by database security rules.
  • Our service providers process data on our behalf only to run the app:
    • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in. Hosted in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1).
    • Google Play and RevenueCat: subscriptions and purchase status. They receive purchase details, not your photos.
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications): receives your notification token and notification text.
    • Email delivery provider: sends your sign-in code.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account removes your profile, photos, captions, hearts, pings and linking requests. Your partner keeps the photos they themselves sent. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days. If you have a subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it; cancel it in Google Play.

If you unlink from your partner, your photos remain in your own gallery until you delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: edit your name and preferences in Settings.
  • Delete: Settings > Delete account, or email us and we will do it for you.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Settings or Android system settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may also have rights to access, export, restrict or object to processing. Email us to exercise them. We respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Photos are stored in private storage, accessible only to the two people in a couple. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password on your email account.

Children

Moments is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. For material changes we will tell you in the app.
