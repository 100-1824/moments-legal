Moments Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026 Contact: momentsapk.app@gmail.com

Moments is a private photo-sharing app for two people. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you can delete it.

The short version

  • Your photos are shared only with the one partner you link to. There is no public feed, no followers and no ads.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.
  • You can take a break or delete your account and all your photos at any time from Settings > Deactivate or delete account.

What we collect

Data Why we need it
Email address To sign you in with a one-time code and to let your partner find you when linking.
Display name and optional partner nickname Shown to you and your partner. The nickname is visible only to you.
Profile photo, birthday and gender (all optional) To complete your profile. Your profile photo is shown to you and your partner.
Photos, captions and short notes you send To show them to your partner and keep them in your shared gallery and notes history.
Chat messages, chat photos and emoji reactions you and your partner send each other To deliver them to your partner and keep your conversation. Only the two of you can read it.
Hearts, pings, social-energy level and game requests (for example asking your partner to play Tic Tac Toe) To deliver these features to your partner.
Time zone To decide which of the three daily slots is open for you.
Purchase status Whether you or your partner has Moments Pro (managed through Google Play and RevenueCat).
Notification token (when notifications are enabled) To send you reminders and alerts about your partner’s activity.
App preferences (accent colour, notification settings, quiet hours) To personalise the app.
App usage and diagnostics (screens and features used, crash reports, device model, Android and app version) To keep the app working, find and fix bugs, and understand which features people use.
Installation ID (a random identifier created by Firebase) To tell installations apart for crash reports and usage statistics. It is not your advertising ID.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising ID. The camera is used only when you take a photo; the photo picker is used only for photos you choose.

Who can see your data

  • Your linked partner sees the photos, captions, notes, chat messages, chat photos, reactions, hearts, pings, social-energy level, profile photo, name and email you share within the couple.
  • No one else in the app can see your content. Access is enforced by database security rules.
  • Our service providers process data on our behalf only to run the app:
    • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in. Hosted in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1).
    • Google Play and RevenueCat: subscriptions and purchase status. They receive purchase details, not your photos.
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications): receives your notification token and notification text.
    • Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config: receive usage events, crash reports and your installation ID. They process this on our behalf to improve and fix the app. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before analytics starts.
    • Email delivery provider: sends your sign-in code.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or show ads in Moments today. If that changes, we will update this policy first. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. If you deactivate (take a break), your account is paused and your data is kept until you come back or delete it. Deleting your account removes your profile, profile photo, photos, captions, notes, chat messages and chat photos you sent, hearts, pings, game requests and linking requests. Your partner keeps the photos they themselves sent. The chat belongs to the couple, so once your account is deleted the conversation closes: your messages and chat photos are deleted, and the messages your partner sent stay stored with their account but are no longer shown to anyone. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days. If you have a subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it; cancel it in Google Play.

If you unlink from your partner, your photos remain in your own gallery until you delete your account.

Chat. Messages and chat photos are kept while your account exists. You can unsend a message or photo you sent: its text and photo are removed for both of you and a “Message unsent” note stays in the conversation.

Usage and crash data is kept by Firebase for a limited period set in Google’s Firebase settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: edit your name, profile photo, birthday, gender and preferences in Settings.
  • Delete: Settings > Deactivate or delete account, or email us and we will do it for you.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Settings or Android system settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may also have rights to access, export, restrict or object to processing. Email us to exercise them. We respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Photos, including chat photos, are stored in private storage, accessible only to the two people in a couple. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password on your email account.

Children

Moments is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. For material changes we will tell you in the app.

Contact
