Child safety standards

Moments is a private photo-sharing app for two people. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any sexualised content involving a minor. This page explains our standards and how to report a concern.

Who can use Moments

  • Moments is for people aged 13 and over. Our Terms of Service do not allow anyone younger to use the app.
  • There is no public feed, no followers, no search for other people and no way to message a stranger. Two people connect only when one enters the other’s exact email address and the other accepts.
  • Photos, captions, notes and chat messages are visible only to the one partner you are linked with.

What we prohibit

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child, in photos, captions, notes or chat.
  • Using Moments to groom, threaten or coerce a minor, or to ask for or share sexual images of a minor.
  • Attempts to link with, contact or pressure someone who has not agreed to it.

How to report a concern

  • In the app: long-press a chat message and tap Report, or open Settings and tap Report a safety concern. This opens an email to us that already includes the details we need.
  • By email: write to momentsapk.app@gmail.com with the subject Child safety.
  • Emergencies: if a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

What we do about reports

  • We review every child safety report and treat it as our highest priority.
  • We remove violating content and suspend or permanently remove the accounts involved.
  • We report apparent CSAM and exploitation to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable, and we cooperate with law enforcement requests.
  • We keep the information needed for an investigation as the law requires, and we never share a report with the person who is reported.

Contact

Our designated contact for child safety is momentsapk.app@gmail.com. We read every message.
