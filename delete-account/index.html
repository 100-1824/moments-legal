Delete your Moments account

You can delete your account and your data yourself in the app, or ask us to do it by email.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Moments and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email apkmoments.com@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: email address, display name, nickname, preferences and notification token
  • All photos and captions you sent
  • Hearts, pings and partner-link requests you made or received
  • Your sign-in

What is kept

  • Your partner’s own photos. The photos they sent stay in their gallery. They are unlinked from you and can link with someone new.
  • Backups. Copies in encrypted backups are removed within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Moments Pro subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store app: Menu > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Moments > Cancel.
